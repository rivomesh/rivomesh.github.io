## The identity

The river otter has a rounded ear, broad muzzle and a tapered tail. Its forward-facing profile and trailing curve suggest steady data flow and give RivoMesh a recognizable silhouette in the console, documentation and app icons.

The horizontal wordmark uses original geometric vector paths and needs no installed fonts. It is ready for use on websites, in documentation and in media coverage.

## Use the identity consistently

- Keep clear space of at least one eighth of the mark's height. Preserve the eye cutout and original proportions.
- Prefer the favicon below 24px. Use the standard mark at 24px or larger, and the horizontal lockup at 128px wide or larger.
- Use blue or ink on light surfaces, and pale blue or white on dark surfaces.
- Flow blue `#2859a6` is the primary color. Signal teal `#247f7c` is a restrained secondary accent. Functional states always include text.
