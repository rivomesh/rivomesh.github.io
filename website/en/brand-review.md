# RivoMesh animal mark design review

## Evaluation and selection

| Criterion | River otter | Kingfisher | Swallow |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Animal cues | Rounded ear, broad muzzle and long tail | Long, pointed bill and diving posture | Swept wings and forked tail |
| Distinctiveness | The river animal connects directly to the Rivo name | Strong direction with a familiar bird profile | The abstract silhouette can suggest an aviation brand |
| Technical character | Continuous curve suits steady delivery and recovery | Sharp profile emphasizes speed | Light silhouette emphasizes fan-out |
| Simplicity | One connected outline with an eye cutout | One outline with an eye cutout | One connected outline |
| Small sizes | Head and tail survive at 16px, with the eye clearer from 24px | The bill remains clear and tail detail merges at 16px | Wing tips survive and species cues soften at 16px |
| Monochrome | Readable from the side in one color | Bill stays visible in reverse | Clean in one color |
| Wordmark harmony | Tail curve echoes the rounded lettering | The diagonal creates stronger visual tension | Wide wings compete for space in the lockup |
| Long-term use | Works as a site, avatar, app and documentation mark | Suits applications that emphasize direction | Suits artwork that emphasizes fan-out |

The river otter is the official mark. Its muzzle, ear and sweeping tail preserve both the animal and the flow metaphor in a single silhouette. [Contact sheet and favicon rasters](/brand/identity-review.png)
